Status: handler not armed

To trigger the dialog, click Arm beforeunload, then either:

  • click Navigate away or Reload, or
  • close the CoreCefView / parent window — this is the path that races with browser teardown and reproduces the crash.

Modern browsers require a user gesture on the page before beforeunload will actually prompt. Click anywhere on the page once after arming, otherwise the prompt is suppressed silently.
